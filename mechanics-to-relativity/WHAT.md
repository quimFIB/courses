A self-study curriculum from Newton's second law to Einstein's field equations: 41 units, theoretical only, each a slide deck with worked examples plus a problem page whose every problem has a hint ladder and a full worked solution folded under it.

`CURRICULUM.html` is the proposal — open it in a browser. The learner it is
written for is mathematically mature (proofs, linear algebra, single-variable
analysis) and has no physics and no vector calculus, ODE methods, Fourier,
complex analysis, PDEs, variational calculus, tensors or differential geometry.
All of those are taught here, physics-first and just-in-time, and no unit may
use a tool before the unit that introduces it. Mathematics carries every
definition and physics the motivation: a tensor is a multilinear map, and the
transformation law of its components is a theorem.

`README.md` is how to use the materials; `AUTHORING.md` is the contract a unit
keeps and its file formats; `BUILD.md` is how the units get built and how to
resume that.

## Loose ends the verifiers raised

- 2026-09-20, **mostly settled: the citations were audited.** 1,461 of them
  across the whole course, against outside sources: 983 confirmed, 33 wrong and
  fixed, the rest unconfirmable from anything reachable online and listed in
  `CITATIONS.md` with what was already tried. Then a second, adversarial pass
  re-derived all 33 fixes from sources it reached *itself* and found **no
  fabrication** — no fix had swapped one remembered locator for another.
  The fixes worth knowing about:
  - `CURRICULUM.html` and `build/syllabus.json` both had Weinberg using
    (+,−,−,−). *Gravitation and Cosmology* uses (−,+,+,+), this course's own
    signature. The page's own warning is that a signature error propagates
    silently from unit 32 to a wrong Mercury number.
  - Wald has no §12.5.2 — that is an equation number. Two agents checked the
    citation and both missed it; a third caught it.
  - Johann Bernoulli's virtual-work letter is **1715**, not 1717; Goldstein's
    Runge–Lenz section is **§9.8**, not §9.7. Both wrong values are the ones in
    common circulation, which is the case for an audit in one line.
  - Ahlfors' winding-number form of Cauchy's formula is §5.1, not §4.5;
    Spivak's divergence theorem is Thm 5-8; Arnold's existence chapter is 3;
    Bradley's aberration is 1728; PSR B1913+16's eccentricity is 0.6171334.
  What is left is in `CITATIONS.md` and needs the books on a shelf. Its
  standing rule: **an unconfirmed citation is left exactly as it stands**,
  because a swapped-in guess looks checked and is worse than an honest gap.
- **The first pass reached for a search engine where a direct source would
  have answered**, and its "unconfirmed" list is padded accordingly — the
  second pass settled 31 of 39 it re-tried, several with one fetch (Michelson
  and Morley's own 1886 paper on Wikisource, CERN's page for the beam energy,
  a full-text Landau and Lifshitz on archive.org). Anyone finishing
  `CITATIONS.md` should start with Crossref, arXiv, ADS and author PDFs.

- The 36–39 verifiers left the same citation gap as their predecessors, and
  named theirs: MTW §35.13–35.15 and §36.11 (unit 38), and the measured values
  in unit 37 (EHT's 48.7 µas shadow, GRAVITY's f = 0.90, Sgr A* at 8.277 kpc)
  and unit 39. These belong in the citation pass above.
- **Declared `needs` lines understate the late units' dependencies.** Unit 40
  declares `needs 36, 15` but genuinely draws on 17, 22, 29, 34, 37, 38 and 39;
  unit 39 declares `35, 22` and cites eight others. Its verifier left this
  alone deliberately — the operative rule is that nothing is used before the
  unit introducing it, which the last units cannot violate, and the specs
  themselves mandate some of the extra material. But the express route is
  computed from those lines, so if a shorter route is ever recut from them it
  will be wrong. The fix belongs in the specs' `needs` lines, not the units.
- 2026-09-20, **settled**: unit 37's imports from unit 36 were re-checked once
  36 existed, and 14 corrections made in 37. The real one: 37 declared "E and L
  are per unit rest mass throughout" and then used the same E and L for light,
  where rest mass is zero — unit 36's derivation never needed the timelike
  normalisation, and 37 now says which parts survive for null geodesics and

  four Killing vectors and 37 said two.
