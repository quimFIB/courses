# Unit 40 — Rotating and charged holes, and where this course stops

- directory: `units/40-rotating-charged-holes/`
- part: Part F — Einstein: the field equations and what they predict (data-trunk="f")
- part thesis: Two derivations of the field equations — vary the Einstein-Hilbert action, or observe with Lovelock that nothing else was ever available — then one exact solution and three numbers. The endpoint is not the equation but the arcseconds: Mercury's 43, the factor of two in light deflection that separates Einstein from a Newtonian corpuscle, and the Shapiro delay. Three branches extend the reach — waves, cosmology, rotating holes — and the last names precisely where this course's methods stop.

## Physics
Kerr: frame dragging, the ergosphere, the two horizons, the Penrose process and its ceiling. Reissner-Nordstrom. No-hair, stated. Gravitational collapse (Oppenheimer-Snyder) and the Penrose diagram of a collapse-formed hole beside the eternal one, with the regions that are not physical identified. Trapped surfaces and the Penrose-Hawking singularity theorems, stated with their energy conditions made explicit because that is precisely where they can fail. The four laws of black-hole mechanics and their thermodynamic reading; the Bekenstein-Hawking entropy and Hawking's temperature — stated as the point where this course's methods run out, since deriving them needs the quantum field theory the course has deliberately not taught.

## Mathematical tools
Killing horizons and surface gravity. The expansion of null congruences and Raychaudhuri's equation, stated and interpreted. Energy conditions (null, weak, strong, dominant) and which known matter violates which. The separability of Kerr geodesics by Hamilton-Jacobi and the resulting Carter constant — the payoff of branch unit 15, and the reason that branch exists at all.

## The worked example the slides are built around
Locate the Kerr ergosphere, show no observer can remain static inside it, and compute the maximum energy extractable from an extremal hole by the Penrose process: 29.3 per cent of its mass. Then separate the Kerr Hamilton-Jacobi equation and exhibit the Carter constant as the fourth constant of motion that makes the system integrable in unit 17's precise sense.

## What the problem sheet exercises
Horizon geometry and conserved quantities. "Carter's constant": separate the Kerr Hamilton-Jacobi equation, identify the fourth constant, and say what unit 17's Liouville-Arnold picture makes of a four-constant system. "Energy conditions": check which conditions a perfect fluid and a cosmological constant satisfy, and locate exactly which hypothesis of the singularity theorem a positive Lambda threatens.

## Checkpoint (falsifiable, and the last slide)
You show that inside the Kerr ergosphere no timelike worldline can hold r, theta and phi constant, compute the Penrose process ceiling, and state the singularity theorem with its energy condition and name a matter content that violates it.
