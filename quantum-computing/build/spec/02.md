## Why this unit exists, and what it must not become
Everything about a single qubit, made geometric. Pure states become points of a sphere, unitaries become rotations of it, and the Hamiltonian of unit 01 becomes an axis and a rate of precession. This is the picture every later unit draws on — Grover's rotation, gate synthesis, the T gate's cost, the transmon's drive — so the map must be proved, not drawn and trusted. The unit also pays unit 00's debt: two bases of ℂ² are mutually unbiased exactly when their Bloch axes are orthogonal, so ℂ² holds exactly three.

It must not become a course on magnetic resonance, Lie theory in general, or spin as angular momentum. SU(2) and SO(3) appear as concrete matrix groups; the Lie algebra is the span of iX, iY, iZ and is used, not developed. Mixed states and the Bloch *ball* are unit 04's — draw the sphere's surface only and say the interior comes later. Two qubits are unit 03's.

## Physics
A spin in a magnetic field precesses, and that is the whole of single-qubit dynamics. Explain from nothing: a proton behaves like a tiny bar magnet; in a field B along z its two states (the Stern–Gerlach outcomes of unit 00) have different energies, H = −(γħB/2) Z with γ the gyromagnetic ratio — ħ restored here, as AUTHORING.md says. Under e^{−iHt/ħ} the Bloch vector rotates about z at the Larmor angular frequency ω₀ = γB (Larmor 1897 for the classical precession; say the quantum statement is the theorem of this unit). The proton: γ/2π = 42.577 MHz/T (CODATA 2018), so ω₀/2π = 63.87 MHz at 1.5 T and 127.73 MHz at 3 T — the clinical MRI frequencies.

Signs: the lab Hamiltonian, the rotating frame and the detuning all carry sign conventions that differ between sources; fix them once on the first slide that needs them, in a `box warn`, and check every later formula against that choice. Driving: add a small field rotating in the xy-plane at frequency ω. In the frame rotating with it the Hamiltonian is time-independent, (Δ/2)Z + (Ω/2)X with detuning Δ = ω₀ − ω and Rabi frequency Ω = γB₁ — the rotating frame derived, not asserted. On resonance the state rotates about x: a π-pulse flips |0⟩ to |1⟩ in time π/Ω, a π/2-pulse makes an equal superposition. Rabi oscillation (Rabi 1937), P₁(t) = (Ω²/Ω′²) sin²(Ω′t/2) with Ω′ = √(Ω² + Δ²). For a linearly polarised drive the counter-rotating half is dropped: the rotating-wave approximation, stated with its condition Ω, |Δ| ≪ ω₀ and its first correction, the Bloch–Siegert shift ≈ Ω²/(4ω₀) (Bloch and Siegert 1940, cited). In MRI, B₁ = 11.74 µT gives Ω/2π ≈ 500 Hz and a π-pulse of 1.0 ms; Ω/ω₀ ≈ 8 × 10⁻⁶, so the approximation's error is about 10⁻¹¹ — and unit 25's transmon is where it stops being negligible. Larmor precession and Rabi oscillation are the two pictures every hardware platform is controlled with; say so.

The 4π fact: a 2π rotation multiplies a spinor by −1. Observable only relative to an unrotated reference: neutron interferometry (Rauch et al., *Phys. Lett. A* 54, 425, 1975; Werner et al., *PRL* 35, 1053, 1975), cited, with what was measured — a precession of 2π in one arm reversing the interference pattern.

## Mathematical tools
The Pauli matrices named (unit 00's X, Y, Z), their algebra: XY = iZ and cyclic, σ_jσ_k = δ_jk I + i ε_jkl σ_l, anticommutation, trace orthogonality tr(σ_jσ_k) = 2δ_jk; {I, X, Y, Z} a basis of 2×2 matrices, Hermitian ones real combinations. The Pauli vector σ = (X, Y, Z) and n·σ for a unit vector n: eigenvalues ±1.

Pure states as ℂP¹: unit vectors modulo phase. The Bloch map |ψ⟩ = cos(θ/2)|0⟩ + e^{iφ} sin(θ/2)|1⟩ ↦ (sin θ cos φ, sin θ sin φ, cos θ), equivalently r = ⟨ψ|σ|ψ⟩, proved a bijection from ℂP¹ onto S² (well-defined on rays; injective; surjective; the poles handled). |ψ⟩⟨ψ| = (I + r·σ)/2. Transition probability |⟨ψ|χ⟩|² = (1 + r·s)/2 — orthogonal states at antipodes, unbiased states at right angles.

Rotations: R_n(θ) = e^{−iθ n·σ/2} = cos(θ/2) I − i sin(θ/2) n·σ (unit 00's functional calculus), and the theorem that conjugation U(r·σ)U† = (Rr)·σ defines a rotation R ∈ SO(3), which for U = R_n(θ) is rotation by θ about n. SU(2) and SO(3) as groups; the homomorphism Φ: SU(2) → SO(3) via conjugation of the Pauli vector, proved surjective (every rotation is about some axis — Euler's rotation theorem, proved or cited from linear algebra over ℝ: a 3×3 orthogonal matrix of determinant one has eigenvalue 1) with kernel {±I} (what commutes with all three Paulis is scalar). Hence SU(2) is a double cover and a 2π rotation is −I. Every 2×2 unitary is e^{iα} times an element of SU(2). The Z-Y-Z decomposition: every single-qubit unitary is e^{iα} R_z(β) R_y(γ) R_z(δ), proved (by the geometry of Euler angles, or directly from the matrix entries). Hamiltonian H = a I + b·σ evolves as precession about b̂ at angular frequency 2|b| — the general statement of which Larmor is an instance.

## The running example
**A proton in an MRI magnet.** Overview: a proton in 1.5 T, the question "what does it do, and how do you flip it?". Section 1 (Pauli algebra): its Hamiltonian written in Paulis. Section 2 (Bloch sphere): its state as a point; the z basis at the poles, unit 00's x and y bases on the equator. Section 3 (rotations): free evolution as rotation about z at 63.87 MHz, drawn. Section 4 (SU(2) → SO(3)): a full 2π of precession returns the Bloch vector but multiplies the state by −1; the neutron experiment. Section 5 (driving): the rotating frame, resonance, the π/2- and π-pulses of an MRI sequence with the B₁ numbers above; the rotating-wave approximation's error in this setting. Close: the pulse sequence as a product of rotations, decomposed Z-Y-Z.

## Worked examples (three to five, in `box ex`)
1. The Bloch vector of (3|0⟩ + 4i|1⟩)/5 (unit 00's test state): (0, 24/25, −7/25), length checked; its antipode and its orthogonal state. Unit 00's notes previewed these three numbers in the order z, x, y — as differences of Stern–Gerlach probabilities, −7/25, 0, 24/25 — so say that this is the same vector, now in the order x, y, z, and that its components are exactly those probability differences.
2. R_n(θ) for n = (1, 0, 1)/√2 and θ = π: it equals (X + Z)/√2 up to the phase −i, the Hadamard gate H of AUTHORING.md's conventions — name it here, and show it swaps the z and x axes.
3. Larmor and Rabi numbers for the proton (63.87 MHz at 1.5 T; the 1.0 ms π-pulse at 11.74 µT), every unit checked.
4. The Z-Y-Z angles of a given unitary, e.g. H, worked fully, with the product multiplied out.
5. (optional) The kernel of Φ computed: U σ_k U† = σ_k for all k forces U = ±I in SU(2).

## What the problem sheet exercises
Ten to twelve problems; at least one proof and one ending in a number; referee checks where natural (axis and angle of a unitary, ZYZ angles).
- "Unit 00's debt": prove two orthonormal bases of ℂ² are mutually unbiased if and only if their Bloch axes are orthogonal, and conclude that ℂ² holds exactly three.
- "Axis and angle": for given 2×2 unitaries — S, T, √X, and a non-obvious one — find the rotation axis and angle, and the global phase discarded.
- "ZYZ angles" for two given unitaries, and the degenerate case (γ = 0) where β and δ are not separately determined.
- "Design a π-pulse": for a qubit with Rabi frequency Ω/2π = 20 MHz on resonance, the π-pulse time (25 ns); with detuning Δ = Ω, the maximum population transferred (½) and the time at which it is reached (π/Ω′ = 17.7 ns).
- "The rotating frame": derive the rotating-frame Hamiltonian from the lab-frame one for a circularly polarised drive, exactly; then for a linearly polarised drive, identify the counter-rotating term and state when it may be dropped.
- "Spinors and rotations": show R_n(2π) = −I for every n and R_n(4π) = I; explain why the −1 is invisible to any measurement on the qubit alone.
- "Surjectivity": a proof that Φ is onto, via R_z and R_y generating SO(3) (Euler angles), independent of the slides' route.
- "Hamiltonians are precessions": for H = a I + b·σ, show e^{−iHt} is a rotation about b̂ by angle 2|b|t up to the phase e^{−iat}; apply to H = X + Z.
- "Cayley–Klein": parametrise SU(2) as {[[α, −β̄],[β, ᾱ]] : |α|² + |β|² = 1}, hence SU(2) ≅ S³ as a set.
- "Unbiased to all three": show that no state of ℂ² is unbiased to all three of z, x, y simultaneously (its Bloch vector would be orthogonal to three orthogonal axes).

## Checkpoint (falsifiable, and the last slide)
Given any 2 × 2 unitary you produce its rotation axis and angle on the Bloch sphere and its ZYZ decomposition, and you prove SU(2) → SO(3) is surjective with kernel {±I}.
